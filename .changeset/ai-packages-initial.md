First release. ai-core: the free-by-default provider chain (Gemini, OpenRouter, NVIDIA, then paid Vertex) with hedging, cooldowns and deadlines, env-driven model resolution, image generation, prompt guards and the shared env schema (ENABLE_BLOG_AI, ENABLE_CHATBOT). blog-kit: full-post, SEO, draft and cover generation, AI images through an ImageSink port, and pure blog utilities. chat-kit: runChat, guarded prompts, output filtering, the knowledge builder, the ChatStore contract and the visitor rate-limit cookie.
